# Privacy policy — Blink Catcher

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Blink Catcher does not collect, send, sell or share any data.

- **What it reads:** the frames of videos that play on `www.instagram.com`, to find inserted
  frames. All analysis runs in your browser, on your device.
- **What it stores:** three settings (on/off, sensitivity, pause on catch) in Chrome's extension
  storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device.
- **Caught frames:** kept only in the memory of the open tab. They are deleted when you close or
  reload the tab. A frame is saved to disk only when you click **Download PNG**.
- **Network:** the extension makes no network requests. It has no analytics, tracking, cookies
  or third-party services.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/angelus169/blink-catcher/issues>.
